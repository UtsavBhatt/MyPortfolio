# I work where finance meets analysis.

I am pursuing an MBA in Finance after a degree in Computer Science. I am interested in how businesses fund themselves, how markets price risk and how better analysis can improve financial decisions.

I work from the underlying information: organising data, checking assumptions, building the model and explaining what the result does—and does not—tell us. I am particularly drawn to fixed income, corporate credit, financial modelling and market research.
