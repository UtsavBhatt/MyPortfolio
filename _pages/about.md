# About me

I’m Utsav, an MBA Finance student with an undergraduate degree in Computer Science. I’m interested in the parts of finance where company fundamentals, capital markets and broader economic forces meet.

My current focus is on fixed income, corporate credit, structured finance and currency markets. I enjoy understanding how a financial instrument works, what drives its risk and return, and how to turn that understanding into a model or a clear piece of research.

I approach projects as practical exercises rather than claims of expertise. I try to show the underlying work—data, assumptions, calculations, visualisations and limitations—so that someone reviewing it can follow the reasoning and form their own view.

## Areas I’m exploring

- **Fixed income and credit:** bond valuation, credit spreads, issuer fundamentals, debt capacity and downside analysis.
- **Currency and derivatives:** USD/INR market structure, futures, options, implied volatility and risk reversals.
- **Corporate finance:** financial statements, integrated modelling, cash flow analysis and capital structure.
- **Technology in finance:** Python-based research workflows, data processing and responsible automation.
