# WayneTech — From Transactions to Financial Statements

A synthetic-company modelling exercise that starts with transaction-level records, classifies accounting entries and builds historical and forecast financial statements.

## Model components

- Raw transaction sheet and processed accounting data.
- Historical financial statements for FY24–FY26.
- Forecast assumptions for revenue growth, cost ratios, depreciation, interest, taxes, capex and borrowings.
- Forecast income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement for FY27–FY29.
- Balance checks across the displayed historical and forecast periods.

## What this model demonstrates

The project focuses on the accounting foundations of financial modelling: how individual transactions are classified, how those classifications feed financial statements, and how forecast assumptions flow through the statements.

It also provides practice in working-capital drivers, fixed assets, depreciation, financing flows and balance-sheet integrity.

## Important qualification

WayneTech is a synthetic company created for modelling practice. Forecasts are outputs of the assumptions entered into the workbook, not forecasts for an actual issuer. The depreciation schedule and selected forecast assumptions require further review before the model is treated as fully validated.
