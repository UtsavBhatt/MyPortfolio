# Projects & Applied Work

A collection of academic projects, independent analyses and practical applications across finance, business analytics and technology.

The objective of these projects is to demonstrate the ability to understand problems, work with data, apply analytical methods and communicate meaningful findings.

---

## Finance & Financial Analysis

### 01. Indian Eurobond Market Analysis

An empirical study of the Indian Eurobond market, examining offshore debt issuance, market structure, pricing dynamics and the broader implications for Indian corporate borrowers.

**Areas covered:** Fixed Income, Corporate Debt, Financial Markets, Data Analysis

**Tools:** Python, Excel

**Deliverables:** Research report, data visualisations and supporting analysis.

### 02. Corporate Credit Analysis

A bottom-up assessment of a corporate borrower from a fixed income investor's perspective.

The analysis focuses on financial performance, leverage, liquidity, debt servicing capacity, cash flow projections and downside scenarios.

**Areas covered:** Credit Analysis, Financial Modelling, Fixed Income

**Tools:** Excel, Python

**Deliverables:** Financial model, credit assessment and investment memorandum.
