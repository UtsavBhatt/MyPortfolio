# NexForge Manufacturing — Three-Statement Model & DCF

A financial-modelling test built around a manufacturing business, linking historical financials, operating assumptions, forecast statements and a discounted cash flow valuation.

## Model structure

The workbook contains dedicated worksheets for assumptions, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement and DCF valuation, alongside a summary and cover sheet.

### Historical and forecast periods

- Historical financials: 2022–2024.
- Forecast period: 2025–2027.
- Operating drivers include revenue growth, COGS, SG&A, depreciation, interest expense and tax.
- Balance-sheet and cash-flow drivers include capital expenditure and debt repayment.

## DCF approach

The valuation builds unlevered free cash flow from EBIT, taxes on EBIT, depreciation and amortisation, capital expenditure and changes in working capital. The projected cash flows are discounted using the stated WACC, with a terminal value calculated using a perpetual-growth assumption.

The submitted model uses:
- WACC: 11.0%.
- Terminal growth: 2.0%.
- Shares outstanding: 20 million.
- FY2024 net debt: US$39 million.

Under those assumptions, the workbook reports an enterprise value of US$879 million, equity value of US$840 million and an implied value of approximately **US$42 per share**.

## What this model demonstrates

- Translating operating assumptions into forecast financial statements.
- Linking the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement.
- Building unlevered free cash flow.
- Applying a DCF framework to estimate enterprise and equity value.
- Documenting assumptions and their rationale.

## Model review notes

The workbook's displayed balance sheet balances across the historical and forecast periods. The cash-flow statement and cash balances should be reconciled further for the historical years, and the valuation is sensitive to the terminal value assumptions. The model is presented as a modelling exercise, not as an audited valuation or investment recommendation.
