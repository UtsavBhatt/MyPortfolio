# Equity Derivatives Ledger

A coursework assignment built around long and short positions in equity options and futures, using contract-level inputs and daily settlement data to track payoffs and mark-to-market movements.

## Workbook structure

- **Overview:** Snapshot of long and short futures and option position results across selected stock tickers.
- **Master data:** Underlying symbols, company names, lot sizes, strikes, option premiums and margin assumptions.
- **Long options ledger:** Call-option premium, expiry spot, intrinsic value and payoff calculations.
- **Short options ledger:** Corresponding short-option position calculations.
- **Long and short futures ledgers:** Contract value, daily mark-to-market, aggregate MTM and margin balance tracking.

## Concepts applied

- Contract size and lot-based exposure.
- Option premium and intrinsic value at expiry.
- Long versus short payoff mechanics.
- Futures settlement and daily mark-to-market.
- Initial and maintenance margin assumptions.

## What this demonstrates

The workbook translates derivative contract mechanics into a structured ledger. It is a practical exercise in tracking how market prices, contract specifications and position direction affect P&L and margin balances.

## Scope

This is an academic assignment, not a live trading record or a recommendation to trade the instruments shown. The workbook's assumptions and illustrative contract inputs should be read in the context of the assignment.
