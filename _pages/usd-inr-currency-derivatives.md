# USD/INR Currency Derivatives Dataset

A historical dataset covering RBI spot rates and exchange-traded USD/INR futures and options from 2018 to 2025. The options dataset contains approximately **1.7 million records**.

Processed fields include implied volatility, option delta, 25-delta risk reversals, tenor buckets and forward points. The spot series also includes daily returns and realised-volatility measures.

The dataset is published on Kaggle with its underlying data and methodology.
