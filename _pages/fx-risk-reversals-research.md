# FX Options as Macro Risk Indicators

This paper tests whether USD/INR 25-delta risk reversals contain information about subsequent spot-market stress. The analysis uses derivatives data from 2018–2025, event windows and lead–lag tests.

<img width="1177" height="615" loading="lazy" alt="USD INR FX options risk reversal research chart" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5cd403ac-ba75-4dbf-b054-ef9e87679f8f" />

**Reported result:** the strongest lead–lag result was observed at a three-day lag (p = 0.009). This is an observational result; it does not establish causality or demonstrate a deployable trading signal.

The research uses a custom dataset assembled from multiple sources and published on Kaggle.
