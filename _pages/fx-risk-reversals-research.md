# FX Options as Macro Risk Indicators

Research question: can USD/INR 25-delta risk reversals signal changing market expectations before stress appears in spot rates? The study uses event windows and lead–lag tests on 2018–2025 derivatives data.

**Reported result:** strongest lead–lag result at a three-day lag (p = 0.009); observational, not proof of causality or predictive performance.
