## Research question

Can USD/INR option risk reversals signal changing market expectations before stress appears in spot rates?

## Method

2018–2025 derivatives dataset · 25-delta risk reversals · Event windows · Lead–lag and Granger-causality tests.

## Reported result

The paper reports its strongest lead–lag result at a three-day lag (p = 0.009). This is an observational result, not proof of causation or reliable out-of-sample prediction.

## Scope

Academic research based on one currency pair. Dataset-count descriptions and reported statistics require final reconciliation before external publication.
